Privacy

We collect nothing.

Last updated 9 October 2026. This covers this website and Margin for Mac and iPad, made by Super Deluxe LLC, California, United States.

This website
It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking code. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps the usual server logs (an address and a time for each request) under GitHub’s own privacy statement. We do not see or keep them.
When you write to us
If you email us, we keep your message and your address so that we can answer you, and use them for nothing else. Ask and we will delete them.
Margin: nothing is collected
Margin has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. It does not track you or keep any record of how you use it, and has no advertising or analytics code in it.
Margin: your writing stays on your device
Everything you write is a file on the Mac or iPad you wrote it on. Margin has no server, and nothing you write is ever sent to us.
Margin: when it goes online
Only where you choose: a reader you ask about your script, a blog you connect and publish to, and a picture you put on a page by its web address. Each talks straight to that service, never through us. Your private notes are never sent to a reader. Dictation uses Apple’s speech service only where the device cannot hear it itself, and no recording is kept.
Third parties
The only third party is a reader or a blog you choose, with your own key, kept in your keychain. There is no data to sell or share, and none is.
Children
We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.
Changes
If anything we make ever sends something off your device, this page changes first, in plain words.
