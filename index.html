Principles

How we build.

01

Native, not web

Built in Swift for Apple's own platforms, so it feels at home and works offline.

02

03

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing leaves unless you send it.

04

Bought, not rented

Pay once for the app. A subscription only for services that cost us to run.
